   1. Release Readiness
* Core workflows validated: registration/OTP, login, browsing content, enrollment/progress tracking, vocabulary review, exam attempts and result review, and admin monitoring.
* Key business rules verified: published/draft/archived state, OTP policy, immutable delivery snapshot, and attempt state constraints.
* Remaining risk is limited to UI accuracy and reporting (Medium defects) and performance improvements (Low).
   1. Recommendation
GO-LIVE (Conditional) - Release 1.0 can be deployed provided that Medium outstanding defects (LE-OPEN-01 to LE-OPEN-03) are resolved in Patch 1.0.1 or mitigated with monitoring and operational controls.
Go-live conditions:
* Fix Medium defects LE-OPEN-01 to LE-OPEN-03 (or deploy with limited scope and hotfix quickly).
* Enable enhanced monitoring for OTP and Attempt flows during the first 1-2 weeks after deployment.
* Track Low defects in backlog and address in the next maintenance iteration.
6. Deviations and Notes
* No scope change: out-of-scope features (e.g., AI grading/proctoring/payment) were not tested, as stated in Test Plan v1.0.
* All test activities were aligned with the approved use cases and SRS without introducing new business flows.
7. Appendix - Scope Traceability (Quick Reference)
Release 1.0 testing scope follows the approved set of use cases, grouped as:
* Admin: Exam catalog management, exam structure editing, attempt monitoring, course/lesson management, deck/card management, speaking and writing exam management.
* End User: Register and verify by OTP, login (password/Google), maintain session and profile, reset password via email OTP.
* Visitor/Learner/Candidate: Browse published exams, browse courses/lessons, enroll and track progress, browse and review vocabulary decks/cards, take exam attempts, review results and history.
* Internal services: Fetch delivery snapshot for attempt delivery.
